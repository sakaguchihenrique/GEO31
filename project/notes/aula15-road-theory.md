## Core Question
How do soil, rock, water, relief, and geological structures affect road design, construction, and maintenance?

## Common Mistakes to Watch
- Explaining roads only as pavement.
- Forgetting drainage.
- Not connecting geology to construction decisions.
- Using a real project example without explaining the technical geology.
